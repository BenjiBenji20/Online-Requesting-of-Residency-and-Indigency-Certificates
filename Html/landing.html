History of Barangay Santulan

A journey through time in our beloved community.

Barangay Santulan has a rich and colorful history that dates back to the Spanish colonial era. Originally a small farming village, it flourished into a vibrant community through generations of hard work, culture, and resilience. The name "Santulan" is said to have originated from the native fruit tree that grew abundantly in the area.

Over the decades, the barangay became a melting pot of traditions, where families shared stories of unity and progress. Today, it stands as a testament to its people's dedication to preserving history while embracing modern development.
