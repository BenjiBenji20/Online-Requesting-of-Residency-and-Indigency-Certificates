Your current approach is already solid, especially with verification via **National ID, name matching, and SMS notifications**. However, to make your system more **realistic and practical for real-world document processing**, consider these suggestions:

### **1. Shift Focus to a Request-Only System**
Your team is right—most barangay-issued documents require a **physical seal and signature**. Instead of issuing the document digitally, make the system **a request management platform** where:
- Residents **submit requests online**.
- Barangay officials **approve and print** the document.
- The system **notifies the resident** when it’s ready for pickup.
  
📌 *Enhancement:* Add an **appointment scheduling feature** to reduce long queues at the barangay hall.

---

### **2. Add More Requestable Documents**
Expand your system to include more barangay documents:
- **Barangay Business Permit/Endorsement** – Often required for small businesses.
- **Barangay Blotter Copy Request** – For reporting and requesting records of incidents.
- **Certificate of Good Moral Character** – Required for job applications or scholarships.
- **Barangay SK Clearance** – For youth leaders participating in government projects.

📌 *Enhancement:* Provide **estimated processing time** for each document request.

---

### **3. Implement a Queue & Processing Status System**
Instead of just confirming the request, allow users to **track the progress** of their document:
✅ **Request Received** – Barangay has received the request.  
🔄 **Processing** – Document is being prepared.  
📌 **Ready for Pickup** – Document is signed, sealed, and ready.  
❌ **Rejected** – If the request is denied (e.g., missing info).  

📌 *Enhancement:* Allow barangay staff to update statuses in an admin dashboard.

---

### **4. Enhance Resident Verification**
Since you’re using a **dummy database**, in a real-world scenario, you’d need access to the barangay’s **official resident database**. If access is limited:
- Require **manual verification by barangay staff** before processing first-time requests.
- Ask for a **selfie upload** with the ID for extra verification (can be reviewed manually).

📌 *Enhancement:* Later on, consider **scanning ID images** for automatic verification.

---

### **5. Add a Digital Payment Feature for Paid Documents**
Some barangay documents require fees. If online payments are allowed in the barangay:
- Implement **GCash, Maya, or online banking** payment options.
- Allow users to **upload proof of payment** if payments are manual (over-the-counter).

📌 *Enhancement:* Provide **auto-generated receipts** for transparency.

---

### **6. Barangay Admin Panel**
Instead of manually tracking requests, barangay staff should have:
- **Dashboard to view requests** (new, processing, completed).
- **Approval/rejection buttons** with reason input.
- **Status update controls** (from pending → processing → ready).
- **Report generation** for tracking request trends.

📌 *Enhancement:* Add **role-based access** (e.g., clerk, secretary, chairman).

---

Your system is already promising! The main improvement is shifting from **document issuance to request management**, making it more **realistic** for actual barangay operations. If you plan to scale this, consider **integrating with government databases in the future** for automated validation.
